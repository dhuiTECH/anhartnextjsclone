162 Main St is a pioneering mixed-income housing development located in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside (DTES). This 6-storey building features 69 self-contained micro-suites, designed to provide dignity and independence for residents ranging from seniors and newcomers to individuals transitioning from shelters.

Innovative in its design, the building utilizes a hybrid structure of light wood frame over a concrete podium. The units were uniquely engineered with flexibility in mind, allowing for potential consolidation into larger family units in the future. This project serves as a model for how affordable rental housing can be delivered on narrow, urban infill sites.
