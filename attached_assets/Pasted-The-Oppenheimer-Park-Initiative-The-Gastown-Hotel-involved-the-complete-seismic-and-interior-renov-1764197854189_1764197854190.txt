The Oppenheimer Park Initiative (The Gastown Hotel) involved the complete seismic and interior renovation of a prominent heritage asset located at 112 Water Street. Situated at the gateway between historic Gastown and the Downtown Eastside, this project revitalized a century-old building into 93 units of safe, supportive housing.

By preserving this critical Single Room Occupancy (SRO) stock, Anhart ensured that vulnerable residents were not displaced by gentrification, while simultaneously restoring the building's historic façade and upgrading life-safety systems.
